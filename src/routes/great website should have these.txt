Your project is not just a website—it's a two-sided marketplace similar to Airbnb, Booking.com, OYO, or MakeMyTrip Hotels. You have three primary stakeholders:

Guests who search and book rooms.
Hotel owners (hosts) who list and manage their properties.
Administrators who oversee the platform.

A production-ready business needs much more than just login and booking. Below is a roadmap of the major components.

A suggested roadmap

Instead of trying to build everything at once, work in phases:

MVP (Minimum Viable Product): Registration, login, hotel listing, room listing, search, booking, payments, admin approval, basic host dashboard.
Production Ready: Reviews, notifications, invoices, analytics, monitoring, backups, security hardening, scalable storage.
Growth: Mobile apps, AI recommendations, loyalty program, referrals, coupons, multi-language support, advanced analytics, and dynamic pricing.

Building a marketplace like this is a substantial project, but by adding features in stages you can launch earlier, gather real user feedback, and improve the platform based on actual usage rather than trying to perfect every feature before release.
